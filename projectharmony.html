SIGNAL GREEN

The Story

The year is 1926. A forgotten station continues to receive a midnight train despite being abandoned for decades. Locals speak of strange whistles echoing through the forest and travelers who vanish without a trace. When the signal turns green, the train arrives. And once it does, there may be no way back.
