物語 | THE STORY

Hidden among mountain forests and overlooking Mount Fuji, Midori Station serves hundreds of passengers every day. Everything appears normal. Tourists arrive. Trains depart. Signals operate on schedule. Until 3:33 AM. When the platform signal turns green. No train exists on the timetable. No operator reports an arrival. Yet a silent train emerges from the darkness. Passengers who board are never seen again. As the station's final night guard, your job is simple. Keep The Signal Green.
