Do not stop the train.

Blackwood Station, 1920. Every midnight, a train emerges from the fog. The problem? Railway records state that the train was permanently discontinued in 1889. Yet it still arrives every night.

You are the night guard of Blackwood Station. Fifteen steps from the platform lies Blackwood Cemetery. When the train approaches, you must keep the signal green. No inspections. No questions. No passengers. And under no circumstances... stop the train.
