物語 | THE STORY

Hidden deep within Japan's mountain forests, Midori Station quietly serves passengers every day. Tourists visit. Residents commute. Mount Fuji watches from the horizon. Everything appears normal. Until 3:33 AM. The station signal suddenly turns green. No train appears on the timetable. No operator reports an arrival. Yet a silent train emerges from the darkness. Passengers who board are never seen again. Tonight, you are the station's final night guard.
