About The Game

Sannata is a first-person horror experience set inside an ancient royal bungalow hidden from the outside world. Rumors speak of a dark entity living within the mansion. People who enter rarely return. Armed only with your courage, you must uncover the truth, complete forbidden rituals, solve mysteries and escape before the nightmare consumes you. Every hallway hides secrets. Every sound creates fear. And every second matters.
