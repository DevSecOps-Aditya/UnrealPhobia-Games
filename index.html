About UnrealPhobia Games

UnrealPhobia Games is an independent game development studio founded by Aditya Singh and co-founded by Devanshu Thapa in India. We specialize in creating immersive horror experiences, psychological thrillers, and atmospheric story-driven games that leave a lasting impact on players.


Our mission is to push indie horror beyond simple jump scares by focusing on strong storytelling, memorable worlds, and unique gameplay concepts such as Signal Green, our upcoming psychological railway horror title.


From abandoned stations and forgotten legends to unexplained mysteries, every UnrealPhobia Games project is designed to make players question what is real and what should have stayed buried. Founded by passionate young developers, we continue to explore new ideas while building original horror universes for players around the world.
